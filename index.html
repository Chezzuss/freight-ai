Pipeline

How it works

  1. 01

    Ingest

    Drop carrier spreadsheets — CSV, TSV, XLSX or XLS — or connect your inbox. Header detection survives shuffled columns, junk preamble rows and messy encodings, and every import returns a row-quality report: dirty-row reasons, invalid-state share, date assumptions.

  2. 02

    Extract & verify

    An LLM maps your columns to canonical freight fields with a confidence score per field, falling back to deterministic heuristics when the LLM is unavailable. Ambiguous day/month dates and invalid US states are flagged — never silently accepted.

  3. 03

    Calculate

    A deterministic cascade resolves the rate per lane: your tenant rate cards first, then lane history inside the 120-day freshness window, then shipped-in defaults. Ocean, air and truck with per-tenant mode gating — and a source label on every hit.

  4. 04

    Human-approved quote

    The calculation persists as a draft; one click promotes it into a real quote in your CRM list with a normalized breakdown. Before anything leaves the building, outbound messages open in preview for review.

Features

What's already built

An engineering inventory, not a roadmap mockup — every item below exists in the codebase today.

GigaParser ingest with quality observability

Four tabular formats with per-format diagnostics, dirty-row reasons, invalid-state and date-assumption markers — verified end-to-end on a real 221-row customer file (211 valid rows).

All-in rates, decomposed

A carrier's single all-in number becomes base freight plus named surcharges — BAF, THC, DOC on ocean; per-kg fuel and security on air; a separate fuel line on truck. Each component is stored on its own and priced into every calculation, so quotes carry a true itemized breakdown.

Ocean, air & truck quote calculator

Per-tenant mode gating before computing, blending lane data, history aggregates and carrier risk. Quotes can originate in the calculator, from email, or by promoting a draft.

Lane analytics when history is sufficient

Pick a lane and see its price series by day, week or month, with average per-km, p25/p75 spreads and sample counts — built from your own committed history, never from data you can't check.

Quote drafts → CRM, in one click

Pricing sessions persist as drafts; one click promotes a draft into a real quote — mode-mapped, breakdown normalized — closing the loop from calculation to sales document.

Cited assistant over your inbox

Inbound email is auto-indexed per tenant; answers carry citation chips and an honest RAG status instead of pretending grounding happened.

Rate answers the LLM didn't invent

The assistant extracts the lane, runs the deterministic rate cascade and quotes the engine's figure with an [R] provenance marker — the model never generates the number.

CRM with follow-ups & pipeline

Deal timelines with notes, calls, open tasks and follow-ups, rendered as a drag-and-drop kanban with a pipeline summary.

AR/AP billing

Invoices with line items and payment records, age-bucketed receivables, plus vendor bills on the payables side.

Carrier recommendations & toll estimation

Truck quotes ship a ranked carrier shortlist — your lane history blended with each carrier's live risk score (failure probability, volatility, per-carrier metrics) — plus tolls via TollGuru with configurable vehicle profiles.

Trust

Trust we can prove — and limits we state

Numbers never appear without a source, failures never masquerade as answers — and the boundaries of the claim are part of the claim.

A source and a freshness check on every rate

Each resolution is labeled tenant_rate_card, lane_history or defaults, with its rate-record ID and valid_from/valid_to window; cards outside their validity window are rejected at lookup time. Lane history older than 120 days is skipped rather than presented as a live rate — the cutoff lives in the query itself, and hits return last-rate dates and sample counts.

The assistant cites, never invents

Answers carry citation chips pointing at the exact email chunks used; rate figures come from the deterministic engine with an [R] marker, and the UI shows the real reason when retrieval wasn't used — instead of pretending grounding happened.

Degraded states are explicit

no_rate_found, unsupported_mode, extraction_unavailable — failures return honest machine-readable reasons and a visible demo-fallback chip, never a silent substitution. The chat degrades; it never fabricates.

A human in the loop, by default

Outbound email defaults to preview-only with a dedicated reply-review page; LLM-repaired rows are marked needs_review; field mappings below 0.60 confidence wait for your confirmation.

Tenant isolation & an audit trail

Every query is tenant-scoped — your brokerage sees only its own data. Six roles gate access, and significant actions land in a tenant-scoped audit log with actor, request context and outcome, browsable in the admin UI.

Known limits, stated up front

No live market-rate feed exists or is promised. PDF attachments aren't ingested yet. Email sync is implemented and unit-tested but demo-grade until configured in production. And "provable trust" today covers the rate-lookup and assistant surfaces — raising the legacy quote circuit to the same standard is tracked in our own code review and closed before release.

FAQ

When does freight.ai launch?

We're pre-release and won't publish a date we can't stand behind. The core is built today — the rate engine with provenance, the ingest pipeline, the cited assistant, CRM and billing — gated by 1,058 passing backend tests in the latest recorded run. What's left before general release is written down in our own code review: raising the legacy quote circuit to the provenance standard, per-tenant mailboxes, PDF ingest. Early-access partners see that list and help set the order.

How much will it cost?

Pricing isn't finalized, so we won't quote numbers yet. Pre-order partners get individual founding terms agreed directly with the founder — before public pricing exists.

Where do the rates come from?

Only from your data: your tenant rate cards, your lane history (up to 120 days old) and shipped-in defaults. There is no live market or spot-index feed — that slot isn't built, and we don't claim it. If there's no data for a lane, you get an honest "no rate found", not a guess.

Does the AI act on its own?

No. It parses, drafts and recommends. Outbound emails default to preview-only, LLM-repaired rows are flagged needs_review, and field mappings below 0.60 confidence wait for your explicit confirmation. AI prepares decisions; your team makes them.

What can I import today?

CSV, TSV, XLSX and XLS spreadsheets, plus email ingest via Gmail OAuth. PDF attachments aren't supported yet — it's on the known-limits list above, not buried in a footnote.

How is my data protected?

Every query is tenant-scoped — your brokerage sees only its own data. Access is gated by six roles (owner, admin, sales, ops, finance, platform super-admin), and significant actions land in a tenant-scoped audit log with actor and request context. PII redaction in logging is live; masking before external LLM calls currently covers column mapping and is being extended — we'd rather tell you where we stand than gloss over it.
